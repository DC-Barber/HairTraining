📋 Developer Formula List
🎓 Formula 1 - Grey Coverage (အဖြူဖုံး) ▶
Main Color (60%) + 2 Level Darker (30%) + Helper (7%) + Intensifier (3%)
➜ Developer: 6% (20 VOL) | Processing: 30-40 min
🎓 Formula 2 - One Color (အရောင်တစ်မျိုးဆိုး) ▶
Color Cream + Developer (1:1 ratio)
➜ Developer: 3%-6% (10-20 VOL) | Processing: 25-35 min
🎓 Formula 3 - Lightening (Level တက်စေရန်) ▶
Bleach Powder + Developer (1:1.5 or 1:2 ratio)
➜ Developer: 9%-12% (30-40 VOL) | Processing: 30-45 min
🎓 Formula 4 - Hot Roots Correction ▶
2 Levels Darker Color OR Add Complementary Color (5-10g)
➜ Developer: 3%-6% (10-20 VOL) | Processing: 10-15 min
🎓 Formula 5 - No Yellow (အဝါသတ်) ▶
Violet (0.66) or Blue (0.88) + Shampoo + 9% Developer (1:1:1)
➜ Apply for 15 min on shampoo bowl
